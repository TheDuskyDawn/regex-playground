Inspect each candidate start position, whether the pattern matches there, and the captures produced. This is a teaching trace—not a view into the engine's private backtracking steps.

Build a trace to begin.

How to read the trace

At each character offset, the debugger attempts a sticky match beginning exactly there. A successful step shows the full match, its range, and capture groups.

A failed step means no match starts at that position. The next step tries the following UTF-16 offset.

JavaScript does not expose its internal backtracking trace. Complex patterns can still take significant time; keep test strings short.
